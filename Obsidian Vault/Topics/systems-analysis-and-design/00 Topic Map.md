# Systems Analysis and Design

## Scope

The systems-development lifecycle from analyst role and project management through requirements, analysis models, design, databases, interfaces, controls, implementation, and support. The anchor textbook predates many current cloud, DevOps, and product-delivery practices.

## Learning target

Move from an ambiguous operational need to traceable requirements, defensible models, coherent design decisions, controlled implementation, and maintainable support while explaining the trade-offs.

## Prerequisites

- Basic software and database concepts
- Familiarity with users, workflows, and organizational constraints
- Ability to distinguish a requirement from a proposed solution
