# Research questions: Computing Research Methods

## Primary question

How should a computing research question be matched to an ethical strategy, evidence-generation method, analysis approach, and defensible presentation?

## Subquestions

- What products can computing research legitimately aim to produce?
- How does a literature review establish the conceptual and evidential starting point?
- When should a project use survey, design-and-creation, experiment, case study, action research, or ethnography?
- How should interviews, observations, questionnaires, and documents generate evidence?
- When are quantitative, qualitative, positivist, interpretive, or critical approaches appropriate?
- What limits must be preserved when presenting conclusions?

## Explicit exclusions

- Treating one textbook as a current institutional ethics policy
- Selecting a method because it is familiar rather than because it fits the claim
- Calling a software build research without an evaluable knowledge contribution

## Evidence needed

- `SRC-0001` for the integrated IS/computing research framework
- Current university ethics rules and assignment rubrics when a real project is planned
- Method-specific primary guidance for high-stakes statistical or qualitative procedures
