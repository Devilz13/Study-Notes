# Misconceptions: Computing Research Methods

Record the incorrect model, why it was tempting, corrective evidence, and a transfer question that distinguishes the models.

| Candidate misconception | Corrective distinction | Transfer check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Building software automatically counts as research | A build becomes research only when tied to a question, method, evidence, and evaluable contribution | What new knowledge would remain if the artefact itself disappeared? |
| A questionnaire is a survey | A questionnaire is one data-generation instrument; a survey is a broader research strategy | Could a survey use records or interviews instead? |
| More data always means stronger evidence | Fitness, validity, sampling, measurement, and analysis matter more than volume alone | Which bias remains even after doubling a convenience sample? |
