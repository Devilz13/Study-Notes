# Concept map: Computing Research Methods

Record meaningful directed relations in prose. Treat AI-generated maps as candidates until their source links are verified.

## Prerequisite relations

- [[Concepts/Research Purpose and the 6Ps]] precedes method selection.
- [[Concepts/Evidence and Literature Review]] establishes what is known and what the project must add.
- [[Concepts/Ethics and Participants]] constrains every strategy involving people or their data.

## Causal or mechanistic relations

- A research question selects and is constrained by [[Concepts/Research Strategies and Methods]].
- Evidence generation supplies material for [[Concepts/Data Analysis and Paradigms]].
- Analysis and limitations determine what [[Concepts/Research Communication]] may responsibly claim.

## Contrasts and boundary cases

- Building an artefact contrasts with evaluating the knowledge contribution of that artefact.
- A research strategy is not the same thing as a data-generation method.
- Statistical significance does not by itself establish practical importance.
