# Computing Research Methods

## Scope

Planning, conducting, evaluating, and presenting research in information systems and computing: research purposes, ethics, literature review, strategies, data collection, analysis, philosophical paradigms, and communication.

## Learning target

Turn a computing problem into a defensible research question, choose a fitting strategy and evidence method, analyze results appropriately, and present limitations without overstating what the study establishes.

## Prerequisites

- A computing topic or practical problem worth investigating
- Basic descriptive statistics
- Ability to distinguish observation from interpretation
