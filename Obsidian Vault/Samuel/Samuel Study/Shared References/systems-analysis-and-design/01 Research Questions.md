# Research questions: Systems Analysis and Design

## Primary question

How can an analyst transform stakeholder needs into traceable requirements and a secure, usable, deployable system design?

## Subquestions

- Which development approach fits the project's uncertainty, risk, and delivery constraints?
- How should requirements be elicited, validated, prioritized, and modeled?
- When do traditional and object-oriented models clarify different aspects of the same system?
- How do architecture, database, UI, interface, control, and security decisions stay traceable to requirements?
- What deployment and support planning prevents design intent from being lost in operation?

## Explicit exclusions

- Treating a diagram as evidence that stakeholder needs are correct
- Assuming the textbook's 2009 delivery practices fully represent current cloud/DevOps work
- Designing production security without current threat, platform, and regulatory sources

## Evidence needed

- `SRC-0001` for lifecycle, requirements, modeling, and design foundations
- Current platform and security documentation for a real implementation
- Stakeholder validation and operational evidence for project-specific requirements
