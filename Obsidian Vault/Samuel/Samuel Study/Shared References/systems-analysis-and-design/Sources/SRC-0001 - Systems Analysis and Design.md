# SRC-0001 - Systems Analysis and Design

## Identity

John W. Satzinger, Robert B. Jackson, and Stephen D. Burd. *Systems Analysis and Design in a Changing World*. Fifth edition. Course Technology / Cengage Learning, 2009. ISBN 978-1-4239-0228-7.

Repository file: [Open PDF on GitHub](https://github.com/Devilz13/Study-Notes/blob/study-memory-structure/Samuel/Samuel%20Study/Shared%20References/systems-analysis-and-design/Sources/Accepted/SRC-0001%20-%20Systems%20Analysis%20and%20Design%20in%20a%20Changing%20World.pdf)

## Role and limits

Anchor textbook for analyst roles, lifecycles, requirements, traditional and object-oriented modeling, design, implementation, and support. Modern cloud, DevOps, product management, and security practices require current supplements.

## Structured extracts

- Part 1: analyst role, development approaches, and project management.
- Part 2: requirements investigation, modeling, alternatives, and traditional/object-oriented approaches.
- Part 3: design principles, use-case realization, databases, UI, interfaces, controls, and security.
- Part 4: operationalization, support, and then-current trends.

These are table-of-contents observations, not yet verified proposition-level notes.
