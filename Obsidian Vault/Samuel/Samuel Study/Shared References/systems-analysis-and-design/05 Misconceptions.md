# Misconceptions: Systems Analysis and Design

Record the incorrect model, why it was tempting, corrective evidence, and a transfer question that distinguishes the models.

| Candidate misconception | Corrective distinction | Transfer check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Requirements are a feature wish list | Requirements need sources, rationale, priority, constraints, acceptance conditions, and traceability | Which stakeholder or operational risk justifies this requirement? |
| UML is the design | A model is a selected representation, not the running system or the whole decision record | What important security or operational property is absent from the diagram? |
| Implementation starts after design is finished | Feedback from feasibility, prototypes, tests, and deployment may revise analysis and design | Which discovery should reopen the requirement? |
