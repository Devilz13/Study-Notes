# SRC-0001 - Researching Information Systems and Computing

## Identity

Briony J. Oates. *Researching Information Systems and Computing*. SAGE Publications, 2006. ISBN 1-4129-0224-X.

Repository file: [Open PDF on GitHub](https://github.com/Devilz13/Study-Notes/blob/study-memory-structure/Samuel/Samuel%20Study/Shared%20References/computing-research-methods/Sources/Accepted/SRC-0001%20-%20Researching%20Information%20Systems%20and%20Computing.pdf)

## Role and limits

Anchor textbook for research design in IS and computing. It integrates research purpose, strategies, methods, analysis, paradigms, ethics, and presentation, but must be supplemented with current institutional policies and newer digital-research guidance.

## Structured extracts

- Chapters 1-6: research framing, process, ethics, Internet research, and literature review.
- Chapters 7-12: surveys, design and creation, experiments, case studies, action research, and ethnography.
- Chapters 13-16: interviews, observations, questionnaires, and documents.
- Chapters 17-20: quantitative/qualitative analysis and philosophical paradigms.
- Chapter 21: research presentation.

These are table-of-contents observations, not yet verified proposition-level notes.
