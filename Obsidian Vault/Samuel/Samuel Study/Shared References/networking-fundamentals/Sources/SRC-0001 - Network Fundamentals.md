# SRC-0001 - Network Fundamentals

## Identity

Mark A. Dye, Rick McDonald, and Antoon W. Rufi. *Network Fundamentals: CCNA Exploration Companion Guide*. Cisco Press, 2008. ISBN 978-1-58713-208-7.

Repository file: [Open PDF on GitHub](https://github.com/Devilz13/Study-Notes/blob/study-memory-structure/Samuel/Samuel%20Study/Shared%20References/networking-fundamentals/Sources/Accepted/SRC-0001%20-%20Network%20Fundamentals%20-%20CCNA%20Exploration%20Companion%20Guide.pdf)

## Role and limits

Anchor textbook for foundational network models, IPv4, Ethernet, cabling, configuration, and testing. Its age and Cisco-course framing limit its authority for modern CCNA objectives, IPv6-first design, wireless, security, and automation.

## Structured extracts

- Chapters 1-2: network communication, protocols, models, addressing, and encapsulation.
- Chapters 3-5: application, transport, and network layer responsibilities.
- Chapter 6: IPv4 addressing, subnetting, ICMP, and an IPv6 overview.
- Chapters 7-9: data link, physical layer, and Ethernet.
- Chapters 10-11: planning, cabling, configuration, and testing.

These are table-of-contents observations, not yet verified proposition-level notes.
