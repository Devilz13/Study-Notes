## Final Cross-Check

- Every comment has a response.
- Every claimed change exists at the stated location.
- Numbers and terminology match across response and manuscript.
- Completed work is distinguished from proposed work.
- Remaining limitations are explicit.
- Venue word/format limits are satisfied.
