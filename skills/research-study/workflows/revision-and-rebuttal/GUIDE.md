# Revision and Rebuttal

Resolve feedback systematically while preserving scientific accuracy. A persuasive response must be supported by the manuscript, completed analysis, or clearly labeled future work.

## Intake

Collect the decision letter, all reviews, manuscript version, supplement, venue response rules, word limit, deadline, and any changes already made. Clarify whether the user wants a plan, response letter, manuscript edits, rebuttal, or all.

## Workflow

1. **Normalize comments.** Split bundled reviews into atomic concerns and remove duplicates while preserving reviewer IDs and wording references.
2. **Classify.** Mark each concern as factual correction, clarification, missing evidence, methodological issue, scope disagreement, presentation issue, or out-of-scope request.
3. **Verify.** Check the paper and sources before agreeing or disputing. Do not dismiss a concern because the reviewer expressed it poorly.
4. **Decide action.** Choose accept/change, clarify, add evidence, narrow claim, respectfully disagree, or acknowledge as a limitation.
5. **Implement or draft.** When editing is authorized, make the change and record exact locations. Otherwise propose text without claiming it was inserted.
6. **Respond.** Use acknowledge → direct answer → evidence/reasoning → exact change/location. Keep responses concise and non-defensive.
7. **Cross-check.** Ensure the response, revised manuscript, figures, tables, supplement, and change log agree.
8. **Re-review.** Reassess whether decision-critical issues are actually resolved and identify remaining risk.

Use [revision-matrix.md](templates/revision-matrix.md) for substantial feedback sets.

## Constraints

- Distinguish completed changes from promised work.
- Do not invent new results, citations, analyses, or page locations.
- Do not overclaim that a concern is “fully addressed” when evidence is partial.
- If reviewers conflict, identify the underlying criterion and use editor/meta-review guidance and official venue policy; do not satisfy both by making contradictory claims.
- Preserve anonymity and confidentiality where required.

## Output Contract

Lead with the revised artifact or response requested. Include the concern matrix, exact manuscript locations, unresolved items, and word-count compliance when relevant. For rebuttals, prioritize decision-critical misunderstandings and evidence over cosmetic replies.
