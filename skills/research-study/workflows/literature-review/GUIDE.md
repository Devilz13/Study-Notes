# Literature Review

Build a traceable synthesis, not a sequence of disconnected paper summaries. Read [research-integrity.md](../../references/research-integrity.md).

## Scope

Clarify or infer:

- research question and intended use;
- discipline and terminology;
- date range, languages, publication types, and databases;
- narrative review, scoped comparison, systematic review, or rapid evidence map;
- inclusion/exclusion criteria and acceptable evidence quality.

If the user asks for an exhaustive or systematic review, state the protocol and limitations before claiming coverage.

## Search and Selection

1. Convert the question into concepts, synonyms, abbreviations, and exclusion terms.
2. Search appropriate primary scholarly sources and field databases. Use current web research when the corpus is not supplied.
3. Apply backward and forward citation chasing to high-relevance seed papers.
4. Deduplicate versions and distinguish preprints from peer-reviewed or updated publications.
5. Record why each source was included or excluded when rigor requires it.

Verify bibliographic details from the paper, publisher, DOI record, or stable repository. A missing search result is not evidence that a paper or idea does not exist.

## Evidence Extraction

Use [evidence-matrix.md](templates/evidence-matrix.md) for substantial reviews. Capture the question, setting, method, data/sample, comparison, key result, uncertainty, limitations, and relevance. Keep claimed results separate from independently checked values.

## Synthesis

Organize around themes, mechanisms, methods, evidence strength, chronology, or competing explanations—whichever best answers the research question. Explicitly surface:

- consensus and robust findings;
- contradictions and plausible reasons for them;
- methodological clusters and tradeoffs;
- underrepresented settings or populations;
- evidence and replication gaps;
- candidate research opportunities, with novelty confidence.

Do not manufacture a “gap” merely because studies use different terminology. Distinguish an important unanswered question from an unstudied but low-value variation.

## Output Contract

Include the scope and search date, search/selection method at the rigor requested, corpus summary, thematic synthesis, evidence-quality caveats, gaps, and a verified bibliography. For paper comparisons, use an exact comparison table before the narrative when several repeated dimensions are known.
