# Paper-Type and Venue Calibration

Read this reference before applying evaluation, validation, or completeness standards.

Venue-specific author and reviewer guidance overrides these defaults. Verify the guidance from the official venue or publisher when a current venue is named.

| Paper type | Primary focus | Validation normally expected | Do not penalize by default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Full research paper | Novelty, soundness, significance, completeness | Strong empirical or theoretical validation, complete positioning, reproducibility | Nothing material merely because it is inconvenient; central claims require support. |
| Extended journal paper | Substantial extension, maturity, completeness | Thorough validation and a clear account of what extends prior versions | Reuse that is transparently disclosed and permitted by the journal. |
| Short paper | One focused, sound contribution | Preliminary but credible support for the core claim | Narrow scope or absence of exhaustive experiments. |
| New-ideas or vision paper | Novelty, motivation, coherence, potential impact | Conceptual argument, feasibility reasoning, or early evidence | Absence of a full experimental campaign. |
| Tool or system demo | Usefulness, maturity, accessibility, architecture, demo scenario | Evidence that the tool works and can be demonstrated | Absence of a large user or real-world validation study unless the venue requires it. |
| Dataset, benchmark, or resource paper | Need, construction quality, documentation, ethics, accessibility, utility | Quality controls, baselines, licensing, maintenance, and responsible data practices | Novel algorithms when the contribution is the resource. |
| Replication or negative-result paper | Fidelity, diagnostic value, transparency | Careful reproduction protocol, uncertainty analysis, and explanation of divergences | Positive results or a new method. |
| Poster or extended abstract | Clarity and early-stage promise | Minimal or preliminary evidence | Limited depth, short related work, or small evaluation. |
| Survey or systematic review | Coverage, method, synthesis, usefulness | Search protocol, selection criteria, quality assessment, and traceable synthesis | A new algorithm or experiment. |

## Venue Context Record

Capture, when relevant:

- venue, year, track, and submission type;
- scope and audience;
- page/word limits and what is excluded from the limit;
- required sections, checklists, artifacts, anonymity, and formatting;
- review dimensions, score scale, and rebuttal rules;
- official guidance URL and date checked.

Do not rely on remembered acceptance rates or score meanings when current official guidance is available. Do not convert between venue score scales without explaining the mapping.
